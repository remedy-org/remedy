# Remedy design system: "Evidence Room"

Approved 7 Nov 2026 from three explored directions (variant B), then made physical the same day ("Evidence Room, made physical": paper objects, stamps, a lit light table). Wave 3 (7 Nov) rebuilt the palette and type as **Lamplight**, the judged winner of three researched directions: a warm graphite room, cream paper and one amber, with Schibsted Grotesk for every word and JetBrains Mono for evidence. The research and judging live in the wave 3 scratchpad (`RESEARCH.md`, `DIRECTION.md`). On 8 Nov the amber was tuned to **brass** and the buttons, nav links and hero laptop were rebuilt ("Lamplight, tuned", the judged winner of three options rendered on the site; a violet accent was tried and left out, because it fought the cream paper and the brass money cues). Later on 8 Nov the de-template pass took out every device first-time reviewers read as "AI-made" (see **No template devices**). It will be refined as we build; changes land here first, then in code. `docs/PRODUCT.md` owns what exists; this file owns how it looks and moves.

## Brief

**The feeling:** an evidence room that runs on trust. Forensic precision, calm, serious enough for a child-safety recall, and premium enough to sell to a company.

| Surface | Archetype | Density | Motion budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage | Marketing, product-first hero, confidence through restraint | Roomy | Exactly four moves: focus-in, one scrubbed sequence, ink, drop (see Motion), on inertial wheel scrolling that stops a fast fling at each main beat (see Motion, Scroll stops) |
| Claim flow | Guided verification (Stripe Identity patterns) | Roomy, one action per screen | State changes, one hand-off (the photo carried from the shutter into the checks) and one arrival moment (the receipt feeding out of the refund row, the PAID stamp landing when PayPal says Received) |
| Seller console | Data, calm density (AG Grid) | Tight | Near zero: panel 160ms, cell flash, row insert |

**One brand hue:** brass, the amber of a manila tag and a brass grommet (calmer than marigold, so it sits with the cream paper as one warm system), and only for "the thing to do now" or "the thing happening now": one amber focal element per viewport (the mark's stub and the focus ring never count). Everything else is warm graphite, and the cream paper.

## No template devices

The rule since 8 Nov 2026, after three rounds of first-time reviewers stalled at 7/10 and rated the product 3/10 on "looks AI-made": each device below reads as a template, not as Remedy. It holds on every surface: the homepage, the claim flow, /try, /signin, the console, Insights and the report.

- **Mono is for data only:** claim ids, codes, CPSC numbers, money in the grid, times, PayPal payout ids, masked accounts. Never a word: not a label, an eyebrow, a column head, a chapter or step name, a readout's name or a button. The word beside the data is the sans ("Code" and then K7Q in mono; "Refund" and then RM-0442).
- **Labels are sentence-case sans:** eyebrows, column heads, readouts, chips on photos and captions are Schibsted at 14px (12px for a caption) in `--muted`, `--text-2` or `--ink-muted`; never uppercase, never tracked (`--text-micro--letter-spacing` is normal; only the dev pages' `.micro` mono caps keep 0.04em). The one uppercase word on the site is the PAID stamp.
- **One ink per heading:** no two-tone headline (a sentence in `--text`, then one in `--muted`), on any surface: a heading is one sentence in one ink, and what would have been its grey half is the lead or body under it. The second tone is for labels only.
- **No decorative order marks:** no zero-padded 01/02 numerals, no lettered badges (A to D), no step meter. Order is shown by position: a list on a hairline, with small nodes that light in turn where it plays. Where a reader has to follow a path, the number belongs to the object: the Problem loop's brass pins, 1 to 4.
- **No pills or chips as labels:** a verdict is a 14px glyph and a word (`VerdictMark`); any other state is a word in its colour, or a sentence on a 2px rule (`Notice`). No dot pills, status chips, boxed timers or sponsor stickers ("Built on AG Grid").
- **Two physical objects:** the paper tag that carries the one-time code (the claim flow's code step, demo photo step and expired card; the homepage's code tag and footer tag) and the paid receipt. Everything else is flat UI on hairlines: offers, notices, the console window's pages, the report (a document in a viewer, not a card on a desk). The QR plate and the CPSC photo plate are cream grounds, not objects; the Problem section's aged sheet is that section's facsimile.
- **One stamp:** PAID, on the paid receipt only (the claim flow's, the Opened Exhibit's and the phone hero's), the one physical moment. In the claim flow it lands only when PayPal reports the payout Received, never beside "On its way". A refusal gets no stamp and no slip: its evidence (the tag, enlarged) is the designed moment. No CUT, HELD or ERROR stamp, and no "ERROR" eyebrow over an error.
- **Level, not tilted:** cards, tags, tiles and windows hang straight. Only a loose object on a surface lies at an angle: the hero's receipt on the light table (-2°) and the Problem sheet on its desk (on a pinned laptop it settles level once open, so its type is crisp).
- **Hairlines over boxes:** sections, panels and groups are parted by space and 1px hairlines, never a box inside a box: no bordered box inside a panel, no card around an offer, no red box around an error, no wrapper border around a grid that already sits in a window.
- **Say each outcome once:** each price, claim id and verdict appears once per view, in bright type where it is the point; the lines around it never repeat it.
- **Fewer type styles:** titles at 500, not 600; no screen renders more than six sizes.

## What we borrow, and from where

These patterns come from measured reference sites (Stripe, Linear, Mercury, Persona, Ramp, Clerk, Stripe Identity, AG Grid demos):

- **Two-tone headline (Stripe):** the claim in full text colour, the explanation in muted.
- **Restraint (Stripe, Ramp):** two weights for most text, few sizes.
- **Real product in the hero (Clerk):** the working claim screen, not an illustration.
- **Evidence next to the verdict (Stripe Identity):** photo beside the verdict and the payout line.
- **Hairlines and ring shadows instead of drop shadows (Linear, Clerk).**
- **Navigation (Linear, Mercury, Vercel):** a flat table of contents in plain words, each section's eyebrow saying the same words; sign-in as quiet text after a hairline divider; one quiet demo button, the short form of the hero's amber action; below lg a Menu word button opens a full-screen native dialog sheet, and the bar names the current section as a breadcrumb where it has room.
- **Grid (AG Grid's own demo, Linear's issue states):** status tabs and two-line cells; a verdict as a glyph and a word, never a dot pill.

**Never:** more than two pinned sequences per page (the second, the Problem sheet's 234vh (134vh held, then 100vh under Fraud checks as it scrolls over), is an exception pending the owner's sign-off; until then it is recorded here and nowhere else); a pinned beat shorter than about 70vh of scroll (the owner found the old 320vh pin "very fast"; the cap is gone, the pacing in `custody/pace.ts` rules, and the Opened Exhibit's stretch, chapters 03 to 05, follows `src/components/home/teardown/README.md`); a pinned frame that is ever empty; a pin without a skip link; a fade-in from black, gradient or acid brand colours, illustration carousels, walls of logos and video tiles, emoji, lorem ipsum, or PayPal's logo (we name PayPal in plain text only); stamps anywhere but the paid receipt; mono for words; zero-padded numerals as decoration; a step meter; a lighter band opening a section after a dark one.

## Type

One voice. Both families are OFL and load with `next/font/google` in `src/app/layout.tsx`:

- **Schibsted Grotesk** for every word: display, headings, body, UI. One variable file, preloaded (it carries the h1, the LCP element). Weights: 400 body, leads and verdict words; 500 display, headings, UI labels and buttons; 600 only for the wordmark and the console ledger's figures.
- **JetBrains Mono** for data only: claim IDs (RM-0442), codes (M4T), recall numbers, masked payer IDs, PayPal payout ids, timestamps, money in the grid, the code on the tag and the PAID stamp. 400 for data, 500 for IDs, the tag code and the stamp. The word before the data ("Code", "Refund", "PayPal payout") is Schibsted. Not preloaded; it swaps in. Every ID and code is `white-space: nowrap`.
- **No serif, anywhere,** with one exception: the Problem sheet's clipping quotes CPSC-2026-0067 in Tinos (Apache 2.0, a Times metric clone, `next/font/google`, not preloaded), scoped to that one facsimile so it reads as a cutting from a federal filing. Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Mono and Instrument Serif are gone, and so is `--font-serif`.

Six steps: 56 / 40 / 22 / 17 / 14 / 12 (plus 28, the 40 step's phone floor and the app's screen titles). No screen renders more than six sizes.

**Figures:** never put `tabular-nums` on Schibsted text: its `tnum` also makes the point and comma tabular ("$29 .99", "19 , 596"). The one exception is a column of whole numbers with no point or comma, where `tnum` touches only digits: the Chain of Custody console's Answered in ("11 s" over "14 s", right-aligned as the console's own column), and the ledgers' whole parts, split from their cents. Tabular figures live only on mono (`code, kbd, samp, pre, .font-mono` get `tabular-nums slashed-zero` in base). Digits that must line up go in mono (`Money size="grid"`, timers); everything else keeps Schibsted's proportional figures.

**Money** (`<Money value={29.99} size="numeral|amount|inline|grid" />`): the whole part, then the cents de-emphasised, as Mercury does.
- `numeral` (display) and `amount`: cents at 55%, raised to the cap height (`vertical-align: .62em`), tight to the whole.
- `inline` (the surrounding text's font) and `grid` (14px mono, tabular): cents at 73%, raised 0.2em.
- Screen readers and copy-paste get the plain "$29.99"; the split parts are hidden from assistive tech.

## Voice and emphasis

Remedy reads like a funded product at launch: confident, plain and specific. Every line says who, what, how fast or how much, and a parent on a phone and a recall manager both understand it read aloud.

**Words:**
- Lead with the outcome ("Every genuine claim paid."), then the support. State the vision in the present tense; never invent users, customers, logos, partnerships or numbers we didn't measure.
- Banned: seamless, leverage, empower, revolutionize, unlock, harness, robust, cutting-edge, game-changer, "in today's world", "not just X, but Y", "it's not X, it's Y", triplets for rhythm, em-dash asides, colon-then-reveal, rhetorical questions as headings, colon-label CTAs ("For companies: run a recall").
- "two minutes" is words in running text; figures (15 s, 1.1¢, $29.99) are numerals.
- Typeset quotes: curly apostrophes and quotes (’ “ ”) in every string a person reads, `&rsquo;` in JSX, never `&apos;`. A reason the rule stores keeps its straight apostrophe (the console's patterns match it) and is typeset where the console, Insights and the report show it (`typeset` in `view.ts`).
- Proof is stated as results a buyer cares about, counted in what was tested (photos, claims, codes) with the tries behind them beside it ("0 of 16 stolen web photos paid, across 48 tries"), never a try counted as a photo; the method, and the one fake the photo check passed, in one footnote: no answer keys, model bake-offs or spike names on the page.
- The four checks before the photo check are the **account checks** wherever a company reads them (grid, panel, Insights, whose Where claims stop names its third stage "Passed the account checks", report, the analyst's field notes). The vision model is named once, in the report's method paragraph.

**Labels (one truth, said once, in one place):**
- No "sandbox", "test", "test mode", "sample", "demo data", "AI-made", "illustration" or "Preview" label on any UI surface, image or OG card. Where a sentence only disclaimed, it is gone.
- The site's one sandbox sentence is in /terms, under Payouts. "PayPal is a trademark of PayPal, Inc." is fine print there too.
- The hackathon is mentioned once: "Started at the PayPal AI Hackathon." at the right of the homepage footer's bottom bar.
- The PayPal login on /try is the **Demo account** ("Use these when PayPal asks you to log in."). On /signin, the For companies door, the shared business login has the same name, **Demo account**, behind one quiet line under Log in with PayPal ("Use the demo account", open from the start when a claim link sent the visitor there), so a company's own PayPal login leads alone; both pages say one consent sentence under the button ("Remedy sees your PayPal name, email, account ID and whether it’s verified, never your password, balance or cards.", `PAYPAL_CONSENT` in `claim/consentCopy.ts`, true to what `lib/paypal/identity.ts` reads; How it works' chapter 01 quotes the same sentence); the word "judge" never renders (its email comes from the server's env, so the business persona needs a neutral address). Claim pages carry no demo label and no guide rail: the Demo account panel and the four claims make the context plain.
- Seeded console claims are **starter claims**; try-page claims are **demo claims**. The starter claims are one dataset, `src/lib/demo/claims.ts` (seven claims), and every picture of them reads it: the hero's proof line, How it works' console, the Opened Exhibit, the For companies tour, /signin, the console and the CPSC report give the same ids, verdicts, codes, amounts, filing times (UTC) and photo-to-payout seconds; `claims.test.ts` fails on any drift. The times are written against `DATASET_NOW` (8 Nov) and the dataset moves forward by whole UTC days to the visit's day (`datasetShift`, never back), so the newest claim is always yesterday's, every time of day stays, none falls before the recall's date, and a page reads the same all day; the console, Insights, the CPSC report and the homepage's tour, Insights and report draft all read that one builder (the homepage regenerates hourly, `revalidate = 3600`), and How it works' chapter 06 reads it too (Hero.tsx draws its queue on the server from the console's rows, and the static figures' RM-0442 row takes its filing day and time from the same rows); only text fixed in the code (`custody/claim.ts` CLAIM.filed, CONSOLE_ROWS) gives times of day alone ("15:08"), no calendar dates. Paid only where PayPal paid: RM-0442, the genuine claim, sent to PayPal 15 s after its photo and confirmed by PayPal at 55 s (the console's timeline: Sent +15 s, PayPal confirmed +55 s). Every homepage line says those two times as sent and confirmed, never "paid 15 s after the photo" (`custody/claim.test.ts`). A claim approved with nothing sent (a held starter claim a person approved) reads Approved everywhere: no payout lines in its history, no share of "paid through PayPal" on the ledger, Insights or the report, whose outcomes list Paid and Approved apart; Insights' Where claims stop stops it one stage short of Paid ("−1 approved · Nothing sent yet"), and the analyst calls a claim paid only when PayPal paid it. No PayPal call is ever made for a starter claim (review.ts), and the panel shows no PayPal ids for one. No surface says "starter", "sample", "demo claim" or "came with your workspace" (Insights' Source for them is "Owner"). A held starter claim's approve says what it records ("Approve RM-9629? Remedy records your decision, and the queue, ledger and report read Approved.", the buttons "Approve…" and "Approve", no amount) and leaves it Approved, never Paid; its decision is kept for the company in its browser (`starter.ts`, the `remedy_decided` cookie, which the server reads): every tab, the ledger, Insights and the report agree, and the shared record stays held for the next company (the shared demo account is every judge's company, so a server-side record would clear the queue for all of them). The console replicas' header (the tour's window, /signin) names the signed-in company "Recall team", never Kikea Store, as `scripts/dev-session.ts` does. - Section names match in the nav, eyebrows and footer: How it works, Why recalls stall, Fraud checks, Results, For companies. Eyebrows are the name alone, never "Exhibit A".
- A product shot carries no sponsor stickers: "Built on AG Grid" and "Built on AG Studio" are gone from the tour's window and the phone story's feet, and no step's sentence names a library (a VP of operations doesn't buy "AG Studio"); the footer credits AG Grid and AG Studio once.

The time from photo to money is "photo to payout" on the ledger and the Insights KPI alike. One time unit: "12 s" in the sans, m:ss only for a running clock.
- Starter claims: one photo and one code per claim. Every photo is the recalled pillow itself, in one of the three prints CPSC's notice names (white with blue flowers and leaves, dark brown with multicoloured feathers, multicoloured puzzle pieces), cut and tagged as the spec asks: CPSC says the pillow carries no label, so the owner ties on a paper tag with X-VESOLJE and the claim's code in marker, and the spec counts no other print and no plain pillow. **Emphasis:**
- Two-tone headlines: the bright `--text` half is the promise or the outcome; the `--muted` half is the support. Each half is at most 34 characters, `display: block`, on the same column start with the same `max-width: 34ch`, so both lines start and wrap alike. Check at 1024, 1280 and 1440px.
- Mono is for codes, ids, amounts and data only (M4T, RM-0442, CPSC 27-071, $29.99 in the grid), never for a word, a label or prose (see No template devices).
- Amber appears in text only as the one action or the one "now" state on a screen.
- Underline is for links only. No italics for emphasis; no bold inside running text.
- The numbers that sell (time, money) sit where the eye lands: the hero facts, the display stat, the receipt amount.

## Colour

Colours are OKLCH tokens in `src/app/globals.css`, exposed to Tailwind through `@theme inline`. **Dark is the default**; light is the designed alternative. A saved choice (the theme toggle, on every page including the homepage) wins; with none saved, a visitor whose system prefers light gets light (the pre-paint script in `layout.tsx`). Token names are stable, so every page follows.

**Guards:** dark neutrals sit at chroma 0.004, hue 75 (above 0.005 the room turns brown and the amber, which shares the hue, stops popping); dark surfaces step by exactly +0.035 L; light neutrals stay at chroma 0.008 or less. A token that differs by theme lives only in the two theme blocks, never in the shared `:root, [data-theme]` block (which comes later and would override them).

## States

Every data view ships all five: empty, loading (skeletons shaped like the content), error (what went wrong and what to do), partial, and success. The claim flow has no skeletons: between its screens the current one stays until the next is ready, with the tapped control busy (label kept, 60%, aria-busy). The money path never silently fails: if PayPal or the model is down, the claim is held and the owner is told so.

## The gate

A screen is done only when it passes the anti-slop gate in the `design` skill:
- grayscale and squint tests;
- spacing on the 4px scale;
- at most 6 sizes and 3 weights;
- real copy;
- all five states;
- works at 375px;
- AA contrast;
- a real dark palette (warm graphite at chroma 0.004, not navy);
- one amber focal element per viewport;
- and it looks like the references, not a template.
